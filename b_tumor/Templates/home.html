Diagnostic Staging Spectrum:
Stage 1
Non-Demented

Normal baseline brain parenchyma with preserved cortical volume and standard ventricles.

Stage 2
Very Mild Impairment

Early MCI transition window. Critical early stage where therapeutic intervention slows progression.

Stage 3
Mild Dementia

Observable memory decline, progressive temporal atrophy, and mild ventricular expansion.

Stage 4
Moderate Dementia

Severe diffuse cortical thinning, pronounced ventricular enlargement, and extensive tissue atrophy.
