About Brain Tumor & Alzheimer Detection Platform

An advanced AI-assisted diagnostic platform leveraging Deep Learning Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for high-accuracy classification and detection of Brain Tumors and Alzheimer's Disease stages from MRI scans.

Key Capabilities

  • Brain Tumor Classification: 4-class multi-class detection (Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary, No Tumor).
  • Alzheimer's Disease Staging: Multi-class staging (Non-Demented, Very Mild, Mild, Moderate Dementia).
  • Clinical Reporting: Automated PDF generation and diagnostic summaries.
