About the CogniScan Platform

Deep learning diagnostic platform focused primarily on Alzheimer's Disease & Cognitive Decline Staging.

Project Mission: The Alzheimer's Motive

Alzheimer's disease detection represents the core research motive of this capstone project. Progressive neurodegenerative diseases inflict irreversible damage to cerebral tissue if not diagnosed in their early transitional phases. Conventional clinical memory tests frequently identify dementia only after extensive cortical degradation has occurred.

This system establishes an automated, objective computer vision pipeline capable of detecting subtle biomarkers—specifically hippocampal volume loss and ventricular enlargement—across 4 distinct stages: Non-Demented, Very Mild Impairment, Mild Dementia, and Moderate Dementia.

Primary Focus: Alzheimer Staging
CORE MODEL

EfficientNetB0 deep transfer learning architecture achieving 95.8% multi-class accuracy and 82.5% dementia sensitivity on holdout test datasets.

Auxiliary: Tumor Screener
SECONDARY

Complementary 4-class CNN (Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary, Normal) retained for auxiliary multi-modal screening.
