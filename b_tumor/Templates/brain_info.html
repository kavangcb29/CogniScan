PRIMARY RESEARCH MOTIVE

Alzheimer's Disease: 4 Clinical Stages

EfficientNetB0 Architecture (95.8%)
1. Non-Demented (Control)
Normal Control

Radiological Correlate: Preserved medial temporal lobe volume, intact hippocampus, and normal ventricle size.

Clinical Plan: Routine annual screening. No therapeutic intervention required.

2. Very Mild Impairment (MCI)
Critical Early Window

Radiological Correlate: Incipient entorhinal cortex thinning and subtle hippocampal shrinkage on high-resolution T1 MRI.

Clinical Plan: Neuropsychological baseline testing, lifestyle modification, and cognitive stimulation.

3. Mild Dementia
Symptomatic

Radiological Correlate: Noticeable ventricular dilation (hydrocephalus ex vacuo) and diffuse temporal-parietal cortical thinning.

Clinical Plan: Cholinesterase inhibitors (e.g. Donepezil), caregiver assistance, and structured daily routines.

4. Moderate Dementia
Advanced Stage

Radiological Correlate: Extensive widespread cerebral atrophy, severely enlarged lateral ventricles, and sulcal widening.

Clinical Plan: Specialized memory care, supportive therapy, and full assisted daily care.
